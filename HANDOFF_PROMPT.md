PROJECT SUMMARY (context, not a substitute for reading the files)
Title: "Beyond In-Domain Accuracy: Auditing Shortcut Learning and Cross-Dataset Generalization in Urdu Misinformation Detection." Short name: Urdu Misinformation Detection: Cross-Dataset Generalization & Shortcut Analysis.
Research question: do Urdu misinformation-detection models learn genuine linguistic signal, or dataset-specific shortcuts (article length, source style)?
Why it matters: Pakistan's fact-checking is documented as English-first while most misinformation consumers are Urdu speakers (a real, sourced service gap); separately, the Urdu-FND literature itself reports 92-98% in-domain accuracy that a July 2026 preprint showed can be a length-confound artifact (one dataset pair, one model; F1 collapses from 0.771 to 0.005 across a transfer direction). The original plain-classifier idea was rejected as redundant with a Feb 2026 paper already at 96.2% accuracy; the project pivoted to auditing that shortcut-learning problem instead — this is the actual novel contribution, not a new SOTA claim.
Datasets: Ax-to-Grind Urdu (primary, 10,083 articles) train; Notri-Fact Urdu (13,388 articles) held out entirely as the cross-dataset test set; never concatenated. UFND (Feb 2026, 14,178 articles) was actively sought as a secondary dataset but its download link could not be located after two independent search passes — formally excluded, not pursued further.
Models: TF-IDF+LogReg, TF-IDF+SVM (classical baselines), mBERT, XLM-RoBERTa-base (primary). No larger/exotic models added — deliberately rejected as disproportionate to the research question.
Experiments (16, IDs A-P in EXPERIMENT_PLAN.md): in-domain baselines, cross-dataset zero-shot transfer (both directions, all 4 models — the core result), length-only baseline, length-ablation replication, length-stratified mitigation + re-test, Integrated-Gradients explainability on a fixed sampled subset, structured error analysis. Primary metric: Macro-F1 (chosen because it penalizes prediction-collapse, which accuracy masks). 3 random seeds for every transformer run.
Product: a responsibly-framed public web app — Urdu/URL input, prediction + "model confidence" (never phrased as probability of falsehood), Integrated-Gradients explanation ("these features contributed," never "this proves it's fake"), plus Methodology/Dataset/Model/Research pages that make the shortcut-learning finding visible to visitors. Deployed live (Vercel + Hugging Face Spaces + HF Model Hub, all free tier).
Objectives this must serve: FYP, undergraduate thesis, possible workshop-tier research paper, GitHub portfolio centerpiece, scholarship-application narrative, AI/ML and software-engineering internship/job portfolio evidence.
